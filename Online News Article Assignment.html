Bonfire Night on February 28

Bonfire Night

A Village Tradition

On the 28 of February, the village will gather for a traditional bonfire. People have been preparing food for days, and there is a lot of excitement in the air. This event is more than just a fire, it is a moment of togetherness

An old villager once said, The fire reminds us that even in the coldest nights, we are not alone. These words are often repeated when the flames rise high into the sky.

According to local stories, the bonfire tradition has been passed down for generations. Some say it protects the village, while others believe it simply brings people closer.

When the fire burns, the past and present meet in its glow. Village Legend
