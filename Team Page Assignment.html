Meet Our Hardworking Team

The green experts keeping the office oxygenated.

Monty the Monstera

Chief Design Officer

Monty has been with the team for three years. He specializes in dramatic leaf splits, occupying too much corner space, and reaching aggressively toward the nearest window. He dislikes cold drafts and direct sunburn.

Sid the Snake Plant

Security & Air Quality Analyst

Sid is the most resilient member of our crew. He thrives under pressure and completely ignores neglect. He works around the clock to filter toxins from the room and prefers to be left alone without water for weeks at a time.

Penelope the Pothos

Growth Specialist

Penelope holds the record for the fastest vertical and horizontal expansion in company history. She loves cascading over bookshelves and trailing down wall hangings. Give her a little water, and she climbs straight to the top.

Sammy the Succulent

Desk Coordinator

Sammy fills a small but crucial niche in our workspace environment. He is low-profile, incredibly compact, and highly aesthetic. He spends most of his days basking in bright sunlight right next to the computer monitor.
