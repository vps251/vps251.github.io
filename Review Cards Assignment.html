Web Design Mastery Flashcards

What does CSS stand for?

Cascading Style Sheets

Which property makes an element 3D?

transform-style: preserve-3d;

What cuts off backgrounds to content?

background-clip: content-box;

How do you separate multiple background URLs?

With commas (,)

What filter renders an image in black & white?

filter: grayscale(100%);

Which axis does skewY() affect?

The Vertical (Y) axis.

What does scaleX(2) do to an item?

Makes it twice as wide.

Does perspective: 100px look extreme or subtle?

Extreme! Smaller values mean closer views.
